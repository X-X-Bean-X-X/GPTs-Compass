# GPT's Compass by X_Bean_X

See map north and your camera heading in Cities: Skylines II.

## Use the compass

- **N** marks map north. A white caret shows your heading while hovering, and a neon-green triangle shows it in the toolbar.
- **Click** the compass to open its menu.
- In **Hovering Compass**, hold **Ctrl** and drag to move.
- Hold **Ctrl + Shift** while hovering to reveal four handles. Drag any corner to resize.
- Position and size save automatically.

## Compass menu

- **Turn North** aligns the camera with map north. It keeps your focus, zoom, and tilt.
- **Return to Previous View** restores the position, zoom, tilt, and heading saved by Turn North.
- **Open Options** opens the mod’s settings.
- **Compass Style** switches between ten designs. **Wealthy** is the default.
- **Compass Placement** switches between the floating compass and the toolbar button.

## Options

- **Enabled** starts on. It controls the compass and its hotkeys.
- **Hovering Compass** floats over the map by default. Move and resize it to suit your screen.
- **Anchored Compass** puts the compass beside the lower toolbar icons. Resizing is disabled.
- **Compass Style** changes the look and saves your choice.
- **Top of Compass is North** keeps the north marker fixed. The heading marker moves.
- **Top of Compass is Current Heading** keeps the heading marker fixed. The north marker moves.
- **Cockpit Display** centers the selected direction and labels its scale in degrees.
- **Hotkeys** has a keyboard and controller field for each action.
- **Turn North** defaults to both stick clicks + **LT**.
- **Return to Previous View** defaults to both stick clicks + **RT**.
- **Open Options** and all keyboard shortcuts start blank.
- Assigned keys and buttons appear beside the actions in the compass menu.
